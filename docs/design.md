# design.md — The Whole Body Architect
## Governing Design Law · Desert Mysticism × Industrial Craft

*A monk's manuscript built with an engineer's hands.*
*Illuminated-manuscript soul, machined precision. The tension between them is the brand.*

---

## 1. THE ONE-SENTENCE FILTER

Every element must belong to a **manuscript**, a **machine shop**, or be cut.
Sacred, but with tolerances. Magic rendered at production quality.

## 2. GEOMETRY LAW

- **The Clover (4+1)** — four heart-leaves in radial cross, single golden stem
  descending to a luminous point — is the founder's emblem and the site's
  operating system. It may appear: as navigation, divider, favicon glyph,
  player turntable, loading state, watermark, seal. It never appears
  decorative-only; wherever the clover lives, it must DO something.
- **The Tetrahedron** — four vertices plus Observer — is reserved exclusively
  for Trust Harness / Design room product content. The two geometries meet
  only inside the Design room, and their meeting there is deliberate theater.
- **Diagonals over grids.** Compositions favor the X-cross, the offset, the
  radical crop. Straight rows are permitted only in the Design room (steel
  discipline) and in tabular résumé data.

## 3. MATERIAL LOGIC — Stained Glass Over Velvet Darkness

Content surfaces behave like panels of glass catching light:

- Faceted edges; internal geometry visible through the pane
- Gold lead-lines delineate structure (thin, precise, #C9A227-family)
- Light comes from WITHIN the content, never from above it
- The base field is deep indigo, never pure black — night sky, not void
- Inner luminescence intensifies under attention (hover/focus = warmth,
  brightness, slow bloom — never scale-jump or snap)

Texture overlays, always subtle, never negotiable:
- Film grain (site-wide, ~2–4% opacity)
- Paper-tooth on reading surfaces (Manuals, essays, long-form)
- Gold-foil accents on Press/Manual language
- No gloss, no neon, no glassmorphism blur, no 3D blob gradients

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY

Two faces. Two registers. Never casually mixed.

- **DISPLAY — an editorial serif with teeth.** High-contrast strokes, chiseled
  geometry, faint art-deco inheritance. Scale is dramatic: 120px+ hero
  display; headlines may clip viewport edges; pillar names set in full-room
  scale. Section numerals old-style, gold.
- **BODY — a warm humanist workhorse.** Invisible, readable, devoted to the
  breath-rhythm copy. Never larger than comfortable; never smaller than
  legible.

Volume law: ENORMOUS and whisper-small, nothing polite-middle. One voice at
full volume per screen; everything else murmurs.

## 5. ROOM TEMPERATURES (the six chambers)

Navigation must feel like walking a house at night, each room with its hearth:

| Room | Pillar | Palette | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water | Music | deep blues, cyan-glass | cool, liquid, resonant |
| Earth | Foundation | ochre, bone, terracotta, dusk orange | the warmest room, grounded |
| Fire | Community | ember orange, rose gold | gathering warmth |
| Air | Manuals | pale silver-violet | spacious, weightless |
| The Hand | Design | near-monochrome steel | boardroom inside the temple |
| Ether | Guardian | pure violet + gold | the sigil's own chamber |

Transitions between rooms are slow crossfades of field hue (1.5–2.5s ease).
Rooms never announce themselves with color shocks; the temperature *settles*.

## 6. MOTION VOCABULARY

One signature, everything else quiet:

- **SIGNATURE: the Clover Bloom.** Four leaves unfold over ~5 seconds
  (coherent-breathing pace) on first arrival; re-triggerable by cursor
  proximity. Under reduced-motion: instant fade-in, full meaning, no motion.
- Ambient drifts: 8–12s cycles, barely perceptible — the house is inhabited
- Scroll reveals: sediment layers, slow cubic ease-outs (400–600ms), never snap
- Cursor: soft violet glow trail on pointer-fine devices; presence, not gimmick
- Hover = warmth. Objects respond to attention like organisms, not buttons.

PROHIBITED: autoplay audio, parallax-for-parallax, bounce easings,
notification-styled motion, anything that performs instead of presences.

## 7. IMAGERY LAW — THE GENERATIVE MANDATE (NO PHOTOGRAPHY)

**Zero photographs. Ever.** The visual world is RENDERED, not recorded.

All imagery is procedural/generated, built from the brand's own systems:

- **The archive is drawn from data.** Desert horizons, flame fields, sound
  spectra, architectural wireframes — each rendered as generative art from
  parameters, not captured by a lens. An Agentic Systems Architect's world
  drawn by the same generative systems he architects daily.
- **Sound becomes sight.** Waveform/spectrum renderings of actual Sandābādo
  audio tracks — each track's visual signature as a violet-gold generative
  artwork in the Water room. The music literally paints its own covers.
- **Terrain as data.** The Foundation room renders the Mojave as a living
  procedural landscape — height-field horizons, dusk-lit, slow-breathing.
  Carrying-capacity and water data, when real, may drive its parameters:
  the land drawn from its own measurements.
- **Fire as particle field.** Community warmth as generative ember particles
  responding to cursor presence — the gathering visualized.
- **Blueprint lineline.** All diagrams (TETRA, trust architectures, site maps)
  drawn in the gold-lineline style with manuscript margins. Precise, glowing
  softly, alive.

Style treatment unified across all renders: desaturated shadow, gold/violet
duotone casting, grain overlay — one continuous universe from sigil to sound
to terrain.

This is the moat: no competitor can copy a founder whose brand world is
generated from his own art, his own data, his own code.

## 8. INTERACTION ROOM SPECS

### The Player (Water room)
Floating dock, bottom-right. Collapsed: single gold point. Playing: the clover
sigil rotating as turntable. Violet filament progress, gold playhead. Track
list expands on hover. Streams only real catalog audio; status language stays
honest ("Catalog active · Debut in preparation"). Opt-in forever.

### The Field Gallery
Generative artworks float at depths in the indigo field — drift on cursor and
scroll, momentum drag with magnetic settle, gold-filament constellation lines
threading each work to its pillar leaf. Hover: warm 1.03 scale, rim-light,
caption rises like liner notes. Keyboard twin: full list view, content parity.

### The Proof Chamber (Design room)
Steel room. Three case studies (TETRA/Trust Harness, Thermo Fisher, Whole
Body Studios) from reconciled data only. Interactive tetrahedron centerpiece —
drag-rotatable, four labeled vertices, Observer vertex pulsing gold, keyboard
twin. Gold-lineline diagrams. Qualified-truth outcomes only.

## 9. PROHIBITION LIST (absolute)

No stock anything. No people-smiling-at-laptops. No 3D blobs. No generic
gradient heroes. No glassmorphism. No neon cyberpunk kitsch. No photography.
No invented metrics, releases, or URLs. No autoplay. No polite-middle type.
No decoration that does not participate in meaning.
